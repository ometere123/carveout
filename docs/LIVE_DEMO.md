# CARVEOUT live demo

The canonical release demo must prove the real trust boundary, not merely show screens.

## Positive path

The provider proposes the complete future SLA, exact exception clauses and structured public-host/path policy while bonding at least the maximum credit. The named customer signs `accept_agreement` before the formation deadline and at least 300 seconds before the SLA window. Only then, after a real completed observation inside that window, open a genuine measured miss with at least two allowed source families and distinct public origins, including an independent probe outside the service domain. Reach independent measurement consensus, invoke a clause already frozen in the accepted agreement, adjudicate from the re-fetched record, resolve a challenge or wait out its window, finalize deterministic credit, and withdraw the customer/provider credits as applicable.

## Negative / fail-closed proof

Also prove a fail-closed or prevention path on the same canonical deployment such as `NOT_PROVEN` measurement, `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` with later dismissal, a rejected exception, a default breach after provider silence, or a challenge that changes the liable share.

## Evidence to record for every write

- exact canonical contract address;
- transaction hash;
- FINALIZED receipt **and** successful execution result;
- relevant finalized state after the write;
- explorer link where available;
- external evidence URLs actually used by consensus.

After the last economic action, record `get_stats()` and show the accounting invariant remains balanced.

Do not fabricate public evidence, change historical dates, weaken source-family requirements, bypass a challenge window, or loosen a frozen rule just to manufacture a successful demonstration. If the chosen example cannot truthfully exercise the positive path, choose a better example.
